Our 2014 choir camp was at Canterbury Cathedral, conducted by Fred Irvine with Dan Soper at the organ.

Highlights of the week included Greene's *Lord, let me know mine end* on the first evening, with the duet sung by two of our younger choristers, Beth and Laura. On the Sunday we sang the Magnificat and Nunc Dimittis in F by Samuel Coleridge-Taylor, a setting new to our repertoire.
