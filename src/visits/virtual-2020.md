Our August 2020 choir camp was due to be at Canterbury Cathedral, but it was cancelled because of the coronavirus pandemic. Instead, the choir worked hard to record a Virtual Choral Evensong, released on Sunday 23 August 2020: the day the camp would have ended, and the Eve of St Bartholomew. The readings, prayers and anthem were chosen for the Eve of St Bartholomew.
